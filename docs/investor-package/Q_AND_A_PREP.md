## 1. Why 80 % equity in the agency instead of 10 %?

Rudi is the sole outside capital source for the agency launch. The 80 % position reflects the full capital risk he is absorbing — entity formation, RERA licensing, office, first agent, marketing, and working capital are all funded by his AED 1 M. In return, 70 % of every agency profit is directed to platform development (which Rudi holds only 10 % of), and the Sunset Clause returns control to founders on the earlier of 2× cumulative return (AED 2,000,000) or 5 years from SAFE execution. The structure rewards the one party actually putting cash in, while keeping founder skin in the game via agency operational control (10 % each) and platform majority (Zhan 80 %).

## 2. What is the Sunset Clause and how does it work?

The Sunset fires on the **earlier** of two triggers: **(a)** cumulative cash distributions paid to Rudi from both Agency and Platform combined reach **AED 2,000,000** (2× the Investment); **OR** **(b)** **five (5) years** have elapsed since SAFE execution. Upon trigger, Rudi's agency equity automatically reduces from 80 % to 10 %. The released 70 percentage points split 35 / 35 between Dymo and Zhan, giving them 45 % each. Platform cap table and the 70 / 10 / 10 / 10 profit split are **unchanged** post-sunset — Rudi keeps his 10 % profit share and his 10 % Platform upside for the lifetime of both entities. The time cap guarantees the control return happens regardless of business velocity.

## 3. What if the agency doesn't earn enough and 2× never returns?

The time trigger (5 years) is the answer. Rudi's 80 % agency position converts to 10 % at the 5-year anniversary **regardless of business performance** — the control return is guaranteed in calendar time, not only in financial outcomes. If the financial trigger fires earlier (cumulative distributions reach AED 2 M before Year 5), sunset happens earlier; if not, it happens at Year 5 exactly. The 1× non-participating liquidation preference separately guarantees that in any sale scenario Rudi recovers his AED 1 M or takes his pro-rata share, whichever is higher. The Platform entity remains a separate lifetime upside at 10 % Rudi. Base case: 8 deals Year 1 at AED 320 k blended commission → ~AED 2.56 M gross; at ~50 % net margin ~AED 1.28 M net; Rudi's 10 % share ≈ AED 128 k per year (base case). Over 5 years that is ~AED 640 k — short of the AED 2 M financial trigger, so the 5-year time cap fires first in the base case. Stress cases (slower growth) do not delay the sunset — the time cap is the backstop.

## 4. What if Dymo or Zhan leaves?

Zhan and Dymo equity vests over 2 years with a 6-month cliff. Departure before month 6 forfeits 100 % of unvested equity back to the Company treasury. Between months 6 and 24, equity vests monthly. Full vesting at month 24. Involuntary termination without Cause triggers single-trigger acceleration. On change of control combined with termination, double-trigger acceleration applies. Rudi's equity is separately fully vested from day one.

## 5. Why 70 % of agency profit to platform development?

The platform is the long-horizon asset — 85 Master Tree modules, 10-year roadmap, the source of defensibility and exit value. The agency is the immediate cash engine but a smaller business by design. Routing 70 % of agency profits to platform development is the financing mechanism: agency funds the platform without Rudi needing to provide separate platform capital. Rudi still participates in platform upside via his 10 % ADGM HoldCo stake. The ratio is fixed in the Shareholders Agreement Dividend Policy — not subject to founder discretion.

## 6. What is the realistic exit scenario and when?

Three non-exclusive paths: **M&A** by a regional incumbent (Property Finder, Bayut, Huspy, PRYPCO-adjacent players) — most likely years 3–5; **Series A / B growth equity** providing secondary liquidity; **IPO** years 5–10 at Master Tree platform maturity. Rudi participates pro-rata in any exit plus his 1× liquidation preference if applicable. On agency-only exit, Rudi's share depends on whether Sunset has triggered (80 % pre-sunset, 10 % post-sunset). On platform-only exit, Rudi holds 10 %. The Sunset mechanism is itself a form of partial exit — Rudi's AED 2 M cumulative return is received in cash distributions over time, independent of any corporate sale.

## 7. What are the tax implications on distributions?

Under UAE Federal Decree-Law 47/2022, the agency pays 9 % Corporate Tax above AED 375 000 taxable income (Small Business Relief available up to AED 3 M revenue). Distributions to shareholders are not taxed at the shareholder level — UAE has no personal income tax. VAT (5 %) applies to commercial brokerage services and is a pass-through. Exact structuring (dividend vs service-agreement flow to Platform HoldCo) is a UAE tax-counsel decision before first distribution. The AED 2 M sunset trigger is measured gross (actual cash to Rudi), tax-agnostic.

## 8. What if we disagree on an operational decision?

Ordinary-course operational decisions are made by the CEO (Zhan) and Operations Principal (Dymo) within their respective domains without requiring shareholder approval. The Board (three directors: Zhan, Dymo, Rudi) resolves by simple majority on matters requiring Board-level decision. Reserved matters — sale of the company, dissolution, SHA amendment — require shareholder approval, where Rudi's 80 % agency vote is operative until Sunset. For platform-entity reserved matters, Zhan's 80 % is operative. Day-to-day disagreement resolution is via Board discussion; unresolved matters escalate to ADGM arbitration.
