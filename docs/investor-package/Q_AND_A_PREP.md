## 1. Why 33.33 % equity each? Why not a different split?

The Agency is structured as an equal three-way partnership because each Party contributes differently but comparably: Rudi provides AED 1 M of launch capital (the entire funding that makes the Agency possible); Dymo provides operational execution, RERA licensing, and day-to-day deal-sourcing leadership; Zhan provides the technology platform, IP, architectural vision, and ongoing Agency technical support. One-third each is the cleanest reflection of a shared-risk, shared-reward model. Neither side is a passive investor — all three are building together. The Platform cap (Zhan 80 % / Dymo 10 % / Rudi 10 %) is separately weighted toward Zhan because he built the entire platform before this deal; Rudi's 10 % is a package grant recognising his Agency funding funds the Platform indirectly via the 70 % profit flow.

## 2. How is profit distributed? What do I actually receive?

Every quarter, after costs, taxes, and a 3-month operating reserve, Agency net profit splits **70 / 10 / 10 / 10** — 70 % to the Platform Development Fund (ADGM HoldCo, which Rudi holds 10 % of), and 10 % each to Rudi, Dymo, Zhan in personal cash distributions. The 70 % inter-company flow is structured as a tax-deductible Service Fee to minimise Agency Corporate Tax. Under Small Business Relief (Year 1 expected, revenue ≤ AED 3 M), the Agency pays 0 % CT. From every AED 1,000,000 of gross Agency profit, Rudi receives AED 100,000 in cash personally plus benefits indirectly from the AED 700,000 funding the Platform he holds 10 % of. UAE has no personal income tax on distributions — Rudi's personal take-home is 100 % of his distributed share, subject only to any foreign withholding based on his tax residence.

## 3. What happens in future funding rounds? Can my stake be diluted?

Two-phase protection. Until the first closing of a Series A round (priced equity financing ≥ USD 2 M led by an institutional investor), Rudi has **weighted-average anti-dilution** — if ZAAHI raises at a lower valuation than the AED 3 M Agency implied at Rudi's Closing, Rudi receives additional shares at no extra cost according to the broad-based weighted-average formula. After Series A, anti-dilution protection terminates and Rudi has **pro-rata participation rights** — he may invest his own capital alongside any new investor at the same price, to maintain his percentage (with a 10 % floor). The structure reflects institutional norms: aggressive early-stage protection transitioning to market-standard participation at Series A and beyond.

## 4. What if Dymo or Zhan leaves?

Zhan and Dymo each subject their Agency AND Platform equity to 2-year reverse vesting with a 6-month cliff. Departure before month 6 forfeits 100 % of their unvested equity back to the Company. Between months 6 and 24, equity vests monthly. Full vesting at month 24. Involuntary termination without Cause triggers 25 % single-trigger acceleration; change of control combined with termination triggers 100 % double-trigger acceleration. Rudi's equity is not subject to vesting (fully vested at Closing) because Rudi provides capital upfront, not labour over time.

## 5. Why 70 % of Agency profit to the Platform?

Three reasons. **Commercial:** the Platform is the long-horizon asset — 85 Master Tree modules, 10-year roadmap, exit value in Years 3–10. The Agency is the immediate cash engine but has a smaller standalone ceiling. Funnelling 70 % of Agency profit into Platform development compounds value over time. **Financial:** Rudi still participates in Platform upside via his 10 % ADGM HoldCo stake — the 70 % flow funds the asset Rudi co-owns. **Tax:** the 70 % flow is structured as a Service Fee from Agency to Platform, deductible at Agency level (reducing Agency CT burden) and taxable at Platform level where QFZP status targets 0 % on qualifying income. Combined effective tax rate on the 70 % flow: 0 % at Agency (via deduction) + 0 % at Platform (if QFZP qualifying) = 0 % combined effective tax. Full detail in `PROFIT_DISTRIBUTION_MECHANICS.md` §3 and §8.

## 6. What is the realistic exit scenario and when?

Three non-exclusive paths. **M&A** by a regional incumbent (Property Finder, Bayut, Huspy, PRYPCO-adjacent players) — most likely Years 3–5. **Series A / B growth equity** rounds providing secondary liquidity opportunities. **IPO** Years 5–10 at Master Tree platform maturity. Rudi participates pro-rata on any exit plus his 1× non-participating liquidation preference (he elects whichever yields the higher return). On Agency-only exit, Rudi's share is his 33.33 % of proceeds (less 1× preference if lower). On Platform-only exit, Rudi holds 10 %. Year 1–2 cash distributions from quarterly dividends provide some direct return independent of corporate sale.

## 7. What are the tax implications? How much actually reaches me?

Three-layer tax-efficient design. **Agency layer** — UAE Corporate Tax 9 % above AED 375,000 taxable income, with Small Business Relief (0 %) in Year 1 if revenue ≤ AED 3 M; the 70 % Service Fee to Platform is deductible, further reducing Agency taxable income; VAT 5 % on brokerage services is pass-through. **Platform layer** — ADGM Qualifying Free Zone Person targets 0 % on qualifying income (inter-company Service Fee income can qualify if transfer-pricing documentation is properly prepared); 9 % on any non-qualifying. **Shareholder layer** — UAE has NO personal income tax and NO withholding tax on domestic dividends. Rudi's foreign tax residence (France or elsewhere, to be confirmed with Rudi's advisor) determines any external withholding. Target combined effective tax burden across the structure: **0–9 %**. From every AED 1,000,000 of gross Agency profit, approximately AED 910,000–1,000,000 flows through to Platform + shareholders after UAE tax.

## 8. What if we disagree on an operational decision?

Three layers of resolution. **Ordinary-course operations** — the CEO/CTO (Zhan) runs Platform technical and strategic decisions; the Operations Principal (Dymo) runs Agency day-to-day commercial decisions. No shareholder approval required for within-budget operational matters. **Board-level decisions** — the three-director Board (Zhan, Dymo, Rudi) votes on cross-entity matters by simple majority (2 of 3). Any two directors agreeing carries. **Reserved matters** (SHA amendment, company sale, dissolution) — require approval by at least two of the three Shareholders, voting by number of Shareholders (not by ownership percentage). With three equal Agency partners, per-Shareholder voting produces the cleanest equal-partner framing: each of Rudi, Dymo, Zhan has one vote on reserved matters. Unresolved disputes escalate to ADGM arbitration.
