## Six areas where your senior eye is the most valuable input

We have flagged these explicitly so you can spend your first review
pass on them rather than re-deriving the rest.

### (a) F2 — Instrument structure: SAFE → loan transition

The package was drafted for a Post-Money SAFE. We have ratified a
pivot to a **loan** structure on 5 May 2026; final terms (principal,
rate, conversion mechanic into the Sunset trigger, secured / unsecured)
finalise at the Rudi meeting **Wednesday 6 May 2026**.

Every SAFE-mention document carries a top-of-doc *Structure Note*
flagging this. Numbers and mechanics remain directionally accurate;
the instrument label and §3 / §4 / §6 conversion clauses in the
TERM_SHEET will rewrite next revision.

**Where your view matters:** loan-vs-SAFE has tax, liquidation-
preference, and convertibility implications under UAE / ADGM
practice. Your read on whether the loan structure should preserve
the AED 2 M cumulative / 5-year Sunset trigger (i.e. trigger
loan→equity conversion) or operate as a discrete repayable
instrument with separate equity grants would shape the rewrite.

### (b) Y1 budget envelope reading — (i) fixed-only or (ii) all-in?

The founder ratified a **Y1 budget envelope of AED 1.5–1.7 M** on
5 May 2026, replacing the prior AED 1.0 M (Rudi's launch tranche).

`docs/viktor-package/CASCADE_FLAGS.md` §3.2 lays out the fork:

- **Reading (i)** — AED 1.5–1.7 M is a fixed-cost-only re-presentation
  of existing P&L OpEx. Variable Cost-of-Revenue (agent commissions
  ~AED 1.17 M Y1, deal-direct costs ~AED 0.63 M Y1) sits above and
  scales with revenue. Reconciles to P&L Y1 total of AED 3.41 M.
  No 437× MOIC recalc needed.
- **Reading (ii)** — AED 1.5–1.7 M is an all-in cash-burn revision.
  Forces document-wide P&L recalc; pushes Y1 EBITDA Margin from
  59 % to ~80 % (probably not credible) and Sunset Financial
  Trigger from mid-Y3 to late-Y2.

**Where your view matters:** which reading would a tier-1 LP read
into the package? We have currently encoded Reading (i) ambiguously
in PITCH_DECK Slide 16 and LAUNCH_PLAN Orientation; either reading
can be tightened. Your call would shape the rewrite.

### (c) Source of the AED 0.5–0.7 M envelope expansion above Rudi's AED 1.0 M

Three plausible sources, none currently encoded:

- Founder cash injection (Zhan / Dymo personal capital)
- First-deal agency revenue reinvested in Months 4–12 (Plot 1 is

- Bridge loan or deferred founder salaries

Your view on which is cleanest from an LP-narrative perspective
would resolve this. Affects FINANCIAL_MODEL Tab 1 §1.5 Capital

### (d) §12(f) Founder authority ceiling — fixed or scaling?

We have just codified joint Zhan + Dymo spending authority at
AED 100 k single-event / AED 500 k cumulative monthly, without
prior Board resolution (TERM_SHEET §12(f), inserted today).

Y1 cash flow stays comfortably below AED 500 k/mo (peak Month 12
≈ AED 470 k); Y2+ ordinary OpEx exceeds AED 500 k/mo. **Open
question:** does the ceiling escalate (e.g., AED 1 M/mo Y2 → AED
2 M/mo Y3+) or stay fixed and require Board recurring pre-approvals
from Y2?

Your view on what is reasonable governance for a controlling-
investor-to-equal-partnership vehicle of this size would resolve it.

### (e) Pressure-test the 437× MOIC trajectory as a senior CFO

The base-case 437× MOIC over 10 years on Rudi's AED 1 M decomposes
into ~AED 115 M Agency + Platform cash distributions (Y1–Y10) plus
~AED 322 M Platform IPO proceeds at Y10 (5.6 B AED IPO valuation,
5.8 % post-Series-C dilution, no Rudi pro-rata participation).

The Rule of 40 trajectory averages 170 over Y2–Y5 (versus the
Series-A benchmark of 30–40). The combined effective tax burden
targets 2–4 % across the structure. The dilution ladder is the
most aggressive assumption and the IPO-multiple compression
(8–9× revenue at Y10 = AED 4.8–7.2 B EV) is industry-standard for
mature PropTech.

**Where your view matters:** what would you stress more or less,

add a Conservative-case MOIC headline figure of ~220× alongside
the 437× to pre-empt the inevitable LP question? Would you
