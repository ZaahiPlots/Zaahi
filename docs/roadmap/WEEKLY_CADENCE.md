## How to use this document

Weeks 1–4 are broken out day-by-day (legal / banking formation is calendar-bound and every day matters). Weeks 5–12 are summarised week-by-week because rhythm settles into a weekly cadence once the regulatory baseline is in place.

Each week lists:
- **Theme** — what this week is about in one line.
- **Monday priorities** — the 1–3 items that must move Monday.
- **Tuesday–Thursday focus** — the body of the week's work.
- **Friday review** — what closes the week.
- **Weekend prep** — what sets up next week.
- **KPIs to track** — measurable outputs.
- **Deliverables** — concrete artefacts.
- **Risks to watch** — specific threats this week.

After Week 12, the cadence transitions to monthly (see `MASTER_IMPLEMENTATION_PLAN.md` §2.5–§2.6).
