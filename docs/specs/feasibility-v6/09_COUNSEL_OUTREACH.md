## Notes on tone (for founder review)

- Both emails open with a human reason for writing (referral / research) — not a generic "We are pleased to introduce".
- Three numbered questions, not five. Counsel triages by question count; five looks like a fishing expedition.
- Budget is stated up front. Firms that won't do AED 35k for a written opinion self-select out, which is the point.
- "We are not yet capital-raising" in Email A — defuses the assumption that we need urgent regulatory cover for an imminent raise.
- Email B is half a beat more formal (Mr., "Best regards") because Kayrouz is older-school and the introduction is colder.
- No mention of the 14th-engine Awqaf question. That is a Zhan-only ratification. Counsel sees it framed as "optionality" only.
- Neither firm is told the other is being approached by name. Standard parallel-RFP hygiene.
