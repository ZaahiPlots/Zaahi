**ICP (Ideal Customer Profile):** UAE mid-tier brokerages.

| Dimension | Target |
|---|---|
| Listings under management | 30-100 active listings |
| Team size | 5-20 agents + 1-2 admins |
| Geographic focus | Dubai primary · Abu Dhabi / Sharjah secondary |

- Property-management-first firms (Asteco · Better Homes PM arm · ServeU) — different primary workflow (leases, maintenance, not transactions).

## §2 Qualification criteria (3-tier · 🟢/🟡/🔴)

### 🟢 STRONG FIT — activate pilot conversation immediately

**All 4 ICP criteria met:**
- [ ] 30-100 active listings currently under management.
- [ ] 5-20 agents on team + 1-2 admins.
- [ ] Revenue AED 5-25M / yr gross commission (validated via public filings OR Dymo market knowledge · estimate acceptable).
- [ ] Actively frustrated with current tool stack (Bayut + Excel + WhatsApp) AND considering platform upgrade within next 6-12 months.

**Plus:**
- [ ] Budget AED 3k/mo confirmed (owner has authority · no 3-committee approval).
- [ ] Decision-maker is the person Dymo talks to.
- [ ] Dubai primary market (priority) · Abu Dhabi or Sharjah acceptable.
- [ ] Prior relationship with Dymo (2+ warm interactions OR inner-circle friendship).

**Action if 🟢:** schedule 30-min in-depth conversation within 2 weeks · pitch pilot · collect commitment signal.

### 🟡 DEVELOP — warm · needs nurture before pilot ask

**2-3 of 4 ICP criteria met:**

Examples:
- Has 50 listings (✓ fit) but team only 3 agents (too small — unlikely to need 5-seat tier · offer individual-broker tier Phase 3).
- Team of 15 (✓ fit) but only 12 listings (too small — developing pipeline).
- Revenue AED 3M (too low at current scale but aiming for AED 8M next year · check back).
- ICP fits perfectly but owner is not actively looking (mid-relationship · not ready for commitment).

**Action if 🟡:** regular touchpoint (coffee per quarter) · maintain relationship · ask light qualification questions · re-evaluate Q2 2027 or Q3 2027.

### 🔴 NOT YET — doesn't meet ICP · keep in CRM for Phase 3

Examples:
- Single-agent operator (use zaahi.io direct · not a Pro tenant).
- Property management-first (wrong workflow).
- Top-10 brokerage (wrong sales cycle · Phase 3+ Enterprise / Custom tier).
- Saudi-only presence (Phase 2 Q2 2027 · not Phase 2 Month 10 launch cohort).
- Revenue below AED 3M/yr (too small for Pro tier pricing · individual ambassador tier only).

**Action if 🔴:** log in CRM · mark "Phase 3 prospect" · no active outreach.

## §5 Qualification questions (15-min coffee call)

**Dymo runs the coffee · asks these 8 questions to assess ICP fit. Structure: 90 seconds per question average.**

### §5.1 Current tools

"What do you use today for deal management, listings, and commission tracking?"

**Looking for:**
- Bayut / Property Finder + Excel + WhatsApp = 🟢 STRONG FIT (our exact ICP).
- CRM like Salesforce / Hubspot = 🟡 DEVELOP (already spending · may stick with incumbent).
- Nothing / fully manual = 🟢 STRONG FIT (easy adoption).
- Custom-built internal system = 🔴 NOT YET (won't switch).

### §5.2 Team size

"How big is your team? Agents, admins, total headcount?"

**Looking for:**
- 5-20 agents + 1-2 admins = 🟢 STRONG FIT.
- 2-4 agents = 🟡 DEVELOP (too small · maybe individual-ambassador tier).
- 20+ agents = 🟡 DEVELOP (may need Enterprise tier future).
- 1-2 agents = 🔴 NOT YET (Pro tier overkill · use free ambassador).

### §5.3 Deal volume

"How many deals do you close per month on average? What's your typical commission?"

**Looking for:**
- 2-5 deals/month at AED 50-200k commission = 🟢 STRONG FIT (Pro tier pays for itself).
- <1 deal/month = 🔴 NOT YET (Pro tier too expensive for volume).
- 10+ deals/month = 🟡 DEVELOP (may need Enterprise tier).

### §5.4 Pain points in operations

"What's the thing that wastes most of your time today? Where do deals fall through cracks?"

**Looking for signals:**
- "Commission tracking is a nightmare" = 🟢 STRONG FIT (Ambassador + Deal Engine direct solve).
- "Clients ask for IRR / feasibility and I Excel it up fresh each time" = 🟢 STRONG FIT (Feasibility Calc solves).
- "We lose leads because follow-up is manual" = 🟢 STRONG FIT (Archibald + Deal Engine solve).
- "Everything works fine" = 🔴 NOT YET (no pain · no purchase).

### §5.5 Budget authority

"Who makes the call on new tools? You alone, or is there a committee / HQ approval?"

**Looking for:**
- Owner decides alone · AED 3k/mo is within discretion = 🟢 STRONG FIT.
- Management meeting · quarterly = 🟡 DEVELOP (longer sales cycle).
- Parent org in London / Mumbai · formal procurement = 🔴 NOT YET (wrong tier).

### §5.6 Timeline

"Are you actively evaluating platforms now, or is this a 2027 project?"

**Looking for:**
- "Now · frustrated with current · ready to switch in Q3 2026" = 🟢 STRONG FIT (pilot candidate).
- "Evaluating end of year" = 🟢 STRONG FIT (Phase 2 timing matches).
- "Maybe 2028" = 🟡 DEVELOP (nurture · check back Q2 2027).
- "Never · happy with what I have" = 🔴 NOT YET (politely exit).

### §5.7 Domain / brand

"Do you have a broker website today on your own domain?"

**Looking for:**
- "No · just Bayut listings" = 🟢 STRONG FIT (Pro custom-domain is upgrade).
- "Yes · WordPress · bad SEO" = 🟢 STRONG FIT (Pro replacement opportunity).
- "Yes · custom-built · happy" = 🟡 DEVELOP (harder switch).

### §5.8 Ambassador network interest

"We have an Ambassador program — brokers invite other brokers, commissions compound across 3 levels. Silver AED 1k, Gold AED 5k, Platinum AED 15k. Interest?"

**Looking for:**
- "Interested · I know lots of other brokers" = 🟢 STRONG FIT (plus Ambassador upside).
- "Maybe · need to understand math" = 🟡 DEVELOP (explain commission mechanics).
- "Not my thing" = 🟢 FIT on Pro tier alone (Ambassador optional).

## §6 Objection handling (5 common objections · pre-prepared responses)

### §6.1 "We already use [competitor platform]"

**Most common for:** HubSpot · Salesforce · Bayut Agent Pro · Property Finder Premium.

**Response:**
> "Fair. Three questions for you:
> 1. Does [competitor] automate commission splits across your agents + ambassadors?
> 2. Does [competitor] have a feasibility calculator your clients trust on the spot?
> 3. Does [competitor] build you a custom-domain website?
> 
> We do all three in one tool. And I'm not asking you to switch today — I'm asking you to try us for 3 months free, see where the gaps are. If at end of pilot, [competitor] still works better — no hard feelings, we learn from your feedback.
> 
> Worst case: you keep [competitor] + gain insight into what's next. Best case: you save on a tool."

**If still no:** accept graciously · mark 🟡 DEVELOP · check back at [competitor renewal date].

### §6.2 "Too expensive for us"

**Most common for:** smaller brokerages · price-sensitive owners.

**Response:**
> "AED 3,000/month · you close one deal — platform pays for a month. You close two — platform pays for the month + half of next. For a 5-agent team, that's AED 600/agent/month. Compare to Bayut Agent Pro at AED 5k + Excel subscription + WhatsApp Business + custom-domain site · you're already paying AED 7-8k/month across 4-5 tools.
>
> But I hear you. How about this: instead of AED 3k, pilot is 3 months free. Month 4 you decide. If you stay, it's AED 3k locked for year 1 — grandfathered forever even if we raise prices to AED 5k later. That's a AED 24k/yr savings vs Month 13 signup.
>
> What part of the value isn't clear?"

**If still no:** offer Silver Ambassador tier only (AED 1k one-time) as entry-level · keeps relationship warm · mark 🟡 DEVELOP.

### §6.3 "We don't need more software"

**Most common for:** old-school brokers · "we close deals on WhatsApp · it works."

**Response:**
> "I respect that. The top 3 Dubai brokerages close 80% of their deals on WhatsApp too. But they have assistants doing the tracking. Do you have that luxury?
>
> Let me show you one screen. [Pull up iPad · show Deal Engine state machine · 15 seconds]. You see where Plot A is. You see the 5 states ahead. You see when NOC was requested. You see the client message from Monday. All in one screen. All automatic.
>
> Compared to scrolling 400 WhatsApp messages to find Monday's client reply. That's 2 minutes of your time, multiplied by 20 deals = 40 minutes/day you don't get back.
>
> Still don't need more software? Genuinely OK. But I'd hate you to be the broker who loses Plot A because WhatsApp moved it below the fold."

**If still no:** accept · this broker will be last to move · but Dymo cannot convince if mindset is fixed. Mark 🔴 NOT YET · revisit when market forces them.

### §6.4 "Let's wait until you have more tenants"

**Most common for:** conservative brokers · "let someone else take the risk."

**Response:**
> "That's exactly why I'm inviting YOU now. I'm hand-picking 3-5 pilots. The people who are there at Month 10 shape the product for the next 5 years. At Month 24, you're just signing up for what someone else built.
>
> Plus: grandfathered pricing. You join at Month 10, you lock AED 3k for year 1. Month 24 signup is AED 5k/month minimum — because by then we have enough data to charge what the market bears. You're paying for your own risk tolerance either way — early = lower price + more say · late = higher price + no influence.
>
> Here's another angle: Rudi Belin already committed AED 1M. The risk of ZAAHI not being viable is already covered by institutional backing. The risk you're taking is 3 months of your time for a free trial. Math check?"

**If still no:** accept · offer to check back at Month 13 when pilot data is public · mark 🟡 DEVELOP.

### §6.5 "Rudi who?" (or any reputation test)

**Most common for:** brokers not plugged into UAE investor scene · doing diligence.

**Response:**
> "Rudi Belin — UAE-based investor · Ambassador tier · Guardian Partner on our board · committed AED 1M institutional backing (wire 2026-05-08). He's written our MOU, TERM_SHEET, reviewed our TERM_SHEET with his legal counsel in April 2026. Full governance setup · board seat · quarterly reviews.
>
> I can connect you with Rudi for 15 minutes if you want a reference call. He'll tell you what he's seeing that made him invest. Genuine transparent. We don't have anything to hide."

**If still no:** accept · maybe Dymo's level of rapport isn't enough yet · mark 🟡 DEVELOP.

**Weekly during Month 10-12:**

- Dymo asks: "What worked this week? What friction? What's missing?"

- Decision point: convert to paid tier OR extend pilot OR exit.
- Expected conversion rate: 50-60% (2-3 of 4 pilots convert to paid AED 3k/mo Pro tier grandfathered).

### §8.5 Conversion to paid tier

**At Month 13:**
- Pilot broker invoiced AED 3k/month · payment Jan 2027 month-start billing.

### §8.6 Ambassador upside from pilot cohort

**Key multiplier:** each pilot broker becomes a Gold Ambassador (part of pilot · AED 5k value included).
- Pilot broker invites other brokers → pilot broker earns Gold tier commissions on L1 (10%) + L2 (4%) + L3 (1%).

- Downline effect: year 2-3 Ambassador network reaches 100+ brokers via pilot-originated referrals.

**This is why pilot cohort is strategic, not just revenue:** pilot brokers seed the entire Phase 2 growth loop.
