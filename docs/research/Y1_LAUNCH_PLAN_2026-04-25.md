## §0 · The honest framing — what AED 1M actually buys

Before any line item, the founder must understand the math. **AED 1,000,000 cannot fund 12 months of "solid UAE company" operations.** Per yesterday's `ZAAHI_LAUNCH_RESEARCH_2026-04-25.md` §6.16, true Y1 fixed-OpEx envelope at "solid" salaries + comprehensive setup is **AED 2.19M – 3.29M**.

**What AED 1M actually does, per the canonical FINANCIAL_MODEL Tab 4:**
- It funds **pre-revenue runway from M1 through M3-M4** (until first deal closes and Plot 9235849 / smaller deals start generating commission revenue).
- Operating breakeven Month 2-3 (FINANCIAL_MODEL); capital breakeven Month 4.
- From M5 onwards, base-case revenue funds OpEx — including normalised founder salaries, brand identity, additional insurance, vehicle, additional hires.

**The plan in this document is therefore:**
1. **Use AED 1M to ignite operations** — buy the licenses, set up the office, equip the team, secure housing, fund founders for the bridge period.
2. **Bridge founder salaries** at TERM_SHEET §15 floor (AED 25-30k/mo each) for M1-M4, normalising to AED 40-50k from M5+ once deal revenue hits.
3. **Defer to Y2 (or fund-from-revenue post-first-deal)** any item where the cost is high relative to its M1-M4 necessity: brand identity, full insurance package, vehicle, DPO retainer, hard-launch marketing.
4. **Honour all founder-confirmed items** — housing AED 250k, G42 Cloud, equipment, medical insurance, visas — within total AED 1M.

This is the only way the math works without deception.
