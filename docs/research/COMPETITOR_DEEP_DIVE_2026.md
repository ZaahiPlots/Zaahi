## Executive takeaways

1. **ZAAHI is not competing with any single player head-on.** Bayut / Property Finder dominate *listings* (our §01 / §02). Huspy dominates *mortgage* (our §22, not yet live). PRYPCO / Propy dominate *tokenisation* (our §35, not yet live). DAMAC / Emaar dominate *development* (our §19, tier-subscribed). Better Homes / Allsopp / E&V dominate *secondary brokerage* (our Agency). Each is a Block of the Master Tree — ZAAHI is the OS across all Blocks.
2. **No competitor has architected a plot-centric graph.** All listings platforms index buildings / units. All tokenisation platforms index buildings. No one indexes **plots** as first-class entities with DDA affection plans, building limits, and 3D massing. This is the ZAAHI-only moat today.
3. **Three competitors worth watching as potential partners, not rivals:** Huspy (mortgage, embed their widget), PRYPCO (tokenisation, channel our plots through their VARA sandbox), Engel & Völkers (luxury brokerage, co-list premium plots with Dymo's network).
4. **Most alarming gap** — Huspy disclosed $270 M in home financing processed with a reported 25–30 % UAE mortgage share *as of 2024 Series B press* (2026 share not yet publicly refreshed); has $100 M+ raised. If Huspy adds a listings portal + 3D, they catch up on our listing moat in 18 months. We need Huspy as a *partner*, not a competitor. See §3 for full framing.
5. **The category isn't a zero-sum fight.** Dubai Q1 2026 transaction volume was AED 252 B (+31 % YoY). Every platform is growing. The question is which ones build infrastructure deep enough to survive the 2028–2030 correction and compound afterward.

## 1. Bayut

### Product
UAE's No. 2–3 property classifieds portal by traffic (~1.7 M monthly visits per Similarweb). Aggregates listings from thousands of brokerages. Property search, map view (2D), price estimation, agent directory, market reports. Features "trusted broker" badge, RERA compliance display.

### Pricing / Revenue model
Listing fees from brokerages (SaaS subscriptions + per-listing credits). No transactional rake. Reported parent-group (Dubizzle Group) H1 2025 revenue: $133 M, up 26.6 % YoY. Full year 2024: $222 M. EBITDA margin ~47 %.

### Funding stage
Owned by Dubizzle Group (EMPG). **Tried Dubai IPO late 2025 offering ~30 % share capital, withdrew days before listing** citing "optimal timing" — in practice, the prospectus showed four years of losses before the 2025 turnaround. Backed by Affinity Partners (Jared Kushner), General Atlantic, KCK Group.

### Geographic focus
UAE primary. Expanding into Saudi Arabia (EMPG has Saudi operations). Parent group operates across MENA + South Asia.

### Recent news 2025–2026
- Delayed IPO late 2025.
- Q1 2026: published market reports flagging AED 682 B full-year 2025 transaction volume, forecast AED 1 T for 2026.
- April 2026: announced trends report ("stability and maturity" narrative).
- Parent Dubizzle Group actively financing Saudi scale-up from UAE cash flow.

### Feature gaps ZAAHI can exploit
- **2D map only.** No 3D. No plot-level geometry. No affection plans.
- **Buildings, not plots.** Their atom is the listing; they have no representation for vacant land plots or plot-level attributes.
- **No AI concierge.** Their chat is generic. No UAE-specific knowledge (Oqood, Ejari, Trakheesi).
- **No tier-subscription ambassador model.** Commodity per-listing fee.
- **No tokenisation, no blockchain.**
- **No feasibility calculator.** Can't serve developers or land investors who think in GFA × sell-price.

### Partnerships we could replicate
- **RERA "first platform fully DLD-compliant"** — Bayut used this as PR. ZAAHI should achieve the same certification Q3 2026.
- **Market report distribution** — Bayut publishes weekly market data; their PR loops this through Gulf News, The National, Gulf Business. ZAAHI can replicate with 114-parcel premium insights (we have the data Bayut doesn't).

### Team / offices
Dubizzle Group HQ in Dubai; team 1,000+ across MENA. Single Dubai office footprint.

## 3. Huspy

### Product
**Mortgage-first super-app for real estate in MENA.** Consumer-facing mortgage pre-approval, agent-facing mortgage origination platform, acquired mortgage brokerages (Just Mortgages UK, Finance Lab UAE). Now positioning as "real estate super-app" — expanding beyond mortgage into listings + transactions.

### Pricing / Revenue model
Commission on each mortgage closed (revenue share with bank partner). SaaS for agent platform.

**Huspy disclosed $270 M in home financing processed (as of 2024 press). Market share claim of 25–30 % dates from 2024 communications; 2026 share may differ.¹ ZAAHI's competitive response remains: partner (Risk 1 mitigation) rather than displace.**

¹ Figures as of Huspy's 2024 Series B press; 2026 market share not yet publicly updated.

### Funding stage
**$59 M Series B (July 2025)** led by Balderton Capital and Peak XV (ex-Sequoia India). Total funding now **>$100 M** across Series A ($37 M, 2022) + Series B. Also backed by Abu Dhabi-based 4DX Ventures, Class 5 Global, BECO Capital, Fintech Collective.

### Geographic focus
UAE core, expanding into Spain (multi-city), Saudi Arabia (2025 entry), and 10-city footprint globally over 4 years.

### Recent news 2025–2026
- **July 2025:** Series B $59 M closed.
- Acquired Just Mortgages (UK) and Finance Lab (UAE) — inorganic roll-up strategy.
- Partnered with Emirates NBD, Mashreq, FAB, and ~20+ banks / lenders.
- Planning Saudi entry + Spain multi-city.

### Feature gaps ZAAHI can exploit
- **No plot-level data, no 3D, no affection plans.**
- **Focuses on mortgage for apartments / townhouses, not land plots or premium off-plan.**
- **No tokenisation, no blockchain.**
- **No ambassador model.**
- **No developer-side tooling** (their primary user is a mortgage-seeking buyer, not a developer).
- **Their map is 2D and is imported from 3rd party.** Weak visual differentiation.

### Partnerships we could replicate
- **Emirates NBD + Mashreq + FAB mortgage partnerships** — these are exactly the banks ZAAHI needs for §22. Huspy proves they'll partner with well-positioned platforms. Relationship path: Dymo's banking contacts → pilot → API.
- **Abu Dhabi-based 4DX Ventures** — a VC ZAAHI should talk to.
- **Balderton + Peak XV** — pre-Series A conversation targets for Platform.

### Risk to ZAAHI
**This is the most strategic competitor.** Huspy's trajectory (super-app, $100 M+ raised, 25 % UAE mortgage share, Saudi expansion) puts them on a collision course with ZAAHI if they add listings + 3D. Three options:

1. **Partner early.** Embed Huspy's mortgage pre-approval widget on ZAAHI plot pages. Revenue share on closed mortgages. This positions ZAAHI as the discovery layer + Huspy as the finance layer.
2. **Outrun on data depth.** Huspy has mortgage; they don't have affection plans, plot geometry, 3D. If we expand 114 → 500 → 1 500 plots with full data, they can't catch up.
3. **Acquisition target.** If Platform reaches Series A at AED 300–500 M valuation, Huspy becomes an acquisition candidate (or vice-versa — we are an acquisition candidate for them).

**Recommendation:** pursue partnership (option 1) in 2026. Keep options 2 and 3 open.

### Team / offices
~300 employees (post-acquisitions). Dubai HQ. Offices in UK, Spain, Saudi (opening). Founders: Jad Antoun (CEO), Khalid Ashmawy (CTO).

## 4. PRYPCO

### Product
**Real estate super-app launched by Dubai-based serial entrepreneur Amira Sajwani (daughter of DAMAC's Hussain Sajwani).** Currently operates PRYPCO Mint — **the MENA region's first government-endorsed tokenised real estate platform**, launched in partnership with DLD, VARA, and UAE Central Bank, with Zand Digital Bank as banking partner.

### Pricing / Revenue model
**Fractional investment from AED 2 000 minimum ticket.** Transaction fee on primary issuance, spread on secondary market, premium subscription tiers. Also runs PRYPCO Blocks (mortgage services), PRYPCO One (luxury concierge), PRYPCO Homes (ready / off-plan listings).

### Funding stage
Self-funded by Sajwani family (pre-PRYPCO sibling of DAMAC); no disclosed external rounds. Strategic government partnership with DLD is worth more than any Series round — they literally run DLD's tokenisation pilot.

### Geographic focus
Dubai exclusive (regulatory positioning).

### Recent news 2025–2026
- **February 20, 2026:** **Secondary market trading for 7.8 M real estate tokens went live on PRYPCO Mint** — AED 5 M+ worth of tokenised properties tradable 24 / 7 via XRP Ledger + Ripple support.
- **DLD tokenisation Phase 2 launched** — secondary market regulated.
- **Market projection:** tokenised assets to represent 7 % of Dubai's real estate market by 2033 (~AED 60 B / $16 B).
- Continues DLD Real Estate Sandbox participation.

### Feature gaps ZAAHI can exploit
- **No plot-level visualisation. No affection plans.**
- **Focused on tokenising *ready properties*, not land plots or off-plan.**
- **No AI concierge.**
- **No agent / broker CRM.**
- **Limited to properties Sajwani family acquires or partners on** — small catalogue (dozens of properties, not thousands).

### Partnerships we could replicate
- **DLD + VARA + Central Bank + DFF (Dubai Future Foundation) Real Estate Sandbox** — ZAAHI must apply to join this sandbox. Path: Dymo → DLD relationship → sandbox application for **plot-level tokenisation** (a category PRYPCO doesn't occupy).
- **Zand Digital Bank** — banking partner for tokenisation flows; relevant to ZAAHI's future Sovereign Bank (§53).
- **XRP Ledger / Ripple** — infrastructure partner; ZAAHI is Polygon + Ethereum-first, but XRPL on the UAE regulatory stack is worth exploring.

### Risk to ZAAHI
**Medium.** PRYPCO owns tokenisation *for ready properties*. ZAAHI should occupy **tokenisation for land + off-plan + fractional JV** — adjacent, not overlapping. Potential partnership: ZAAHI provides the plot graph; PRYPCO tokenises select plots as a service.

### Team / offices
Small team (dozens). Dubai HQ. Founder: Amira Sajwani.

## 5. Propy

### Product
**US-originated blockchain real estate transaction platform.** Automates end-to-end real estate closings with smart contracts, title & escrow operations, NFT deed issuance. Not UAE-focused — US primary market, expanding via AI-led acquisition roll-up of title / escrow companies.

### Pricing / Revenue model
SaaS subscription for agents + per-transaction fee. Acquires title & escrow companies to vertically integrate. Target: fragmented $25 B US title + escrow industry.

### Funding stage
**$100 M credit facility from Metropolitan Partners Group (January 29, 2026).** Earlier backing from Tim Draper and others. Tokenised project via PRO Token.

### Geographic focus
US primary. Global aspirations via NFT deeds but limited real traction outside US.

### Recent news 2025–2026
- **January 29, 2026:** $100 M credit facility announced.
- Partnership with Parcl (on-chain real estate analytics, July 2024).
- PropTech Connect 2026 Dubai conference participation.
- Pilot programs with government authorities (unspecified; likely US county-level).

### Feature gaps ZAAHI can exploit
- **Virtually no UAE presence.** Their NFT / blockchain title work is US-focused.
- **Not integrated with DLD or VARA.**
- **No plot geometry, no 3D, no affection plans.**
- **Language / compliance gap** — their platform is English-only, US legal framework.

### Partnerships we could replicate
- **Title + escrow vertical integration play** — ZAAHI's §10 Insurance + §32 Escrow could replicate Propy's model in UAE: acquire / partner a DDA-registered escrow provider and a DLD-approved title insurance broker.
- **NFT deed infrastructure** — Propy's smart contracts are a reference architecture for §35 Tokenisation Phase 2.

### Risk to ZAAHI
**Low.** Propy is US-focused. Only overlap is philosophical (blockchain-first). No direct competition in UAE.

### Team / offices
~200 employees. San Francisco HQ. Ukraine engineering office. Founder: Natalia Karayaneva.

### Product
**Primary: private real estate developer** — ultra-luxury residential / hospitality / branded (Cavalli Tower, DAMAC Lagoons, Riverside). Secondary: in-house sales / brokerage team that sells DAMAC inventory + selectively resells secondary. Not a horizontal brokerage like Better Homes.

### Pricing / Revenue model
Off-plan sales + handover + yield on retained hospitality units. Not a platform / not a SaaS. Revenue = development sales.

### Funding stage
Public → privatised. Currently private, ultra-large balance sheet. Sajwani family controlled.

### Geographic focus
Dubai primary; London, Saudi, Maldives projects; Abu Dhabi.

### Recent news 2025–2026
- **March 2026:** **AED 3.12 B sales in a single month across 1 106 transactions.** Q1 2026: 3 663 units sold.
- **2025 total:** AED 36 B sales.
- Positioned as No. 1 developer by Dubai monthly transaction volume, ahead of Emaar in March 2026.
- Consistently expanding inventory (branded residences, hospitality).

### Feature gaps ZAAHI can exploit
- **Closed ecosystem.** DAMAC's brokerage arm sells DAMAC inventory only. They don't cross-list, don't aggregate secondary market, don't serve other developers.
- **No tech platform.** Their sales happen via WhatsApp, Excel, in-person. ZAAHI can be DAMAC's *tech layer* (developer subscription AED 50 k / yr).
- **No plot-level exposure** — they transact in units, not land.

### Partnerships we could replicate
- **Developer subscription tier** — if we can land DAMAC on the Developer tier (AED 50 k / yr) as a reference, every smaller developer follows. Route via Dymo → Sajwani family contacts (his network overlap with Dubai developer circle).
- **Cross-listing** — ZAAHI Platform lists DAMAC off-plan inventory for ZAAHI users; DAMAC's in-house sales team takes leads.

### Risk to ZAAHI
**None directly.** DAMAC is a developer, not a platform competitor. They are **a customer** on the Platform.

### Team / offices
~2 500 employees total (inc. construction, hospitality). Dubai HQ. International offices London, Riyadh, Doha.
