# Ubora Towers — research notes

**Architect:** Aedas, lead designer Andrew Bromberg.
**Developer:** Ubora Towers FZ LLC.
**Completion:** 2010–2011.
**Coordinates:** 25.1805778°N, 55.2710278°E (Wikipedia infobox — exact).

## Two towers

- **Tower 1 (Commercial):** 263 m / 58 floors / 862 ft. Aedas design.
- **Tower 2 (Residential):** ~70 m / 20 floors.

## Shape

- Aedas / Bromberg house style: angular, clean glass massing with subtle
  setbacks. Standard rectangular slab with a slight asymmetric crown.
- No void / no twist / no extreme features — recognisable mainly by
  position (Business Bay corner) and the two-tower configuration.

## v3 modelling approach

- T1: 40 × 40 × 263 m rectangular slab + standard 3-tier crown.
- T2: 32 × 28 × 70 m, simpler 2-tier.
- Per-floor spandrels + vertical mullions on T1's broad face.
- Glass: standard dark blue-grey (no special override needed).
