# Churchill Tower (Churchill Residence) — research notes

**Architect:** DAR — Design and Architecture Bureau.
**Developer:** Dubai Properties.
**Completion:** 2010.
**Height:** 235 m roof; 61 floors.
**Use:** Residential. Mixed-use complex with the 42-storey ENI commercial building (Eni residence + Churchill share a podium).
**Location:** "346–563 Al Amal Street", Business Bay, along Dubai Canal. Approx 25.184°N, 55.265°E.

## Shape (key distinctive feature)

- **"Resembles New York City's Chrysler Building, and draws upon its Art Deco facade"** — *Wikipedia*
- **"Sailboat-shaped skyscraper"** — *Dubai Property Guide*
- Stepped Art Deco crown — the top has set-back tiers that taper as they rise.
- The companion ENI tower has a contrasting majority-opaque facade ("alternating details" — one is mostly glass, the other mostly opaque).
- "Connected via an elevated outdoor Podium area housing most amenities."

## v3 modelling approach

- Standard rectangular slab body up to ~205 m.
- 3-step Art Deco crown from 205 → 235 m, each step ~50 % smaller than the last.
- Slight curve / sweep to one face approximating the "sailboat" description.
- Warm beige concrete material for the crown (Chrysler-style stone) vs blue-grey
  glass for the body.
